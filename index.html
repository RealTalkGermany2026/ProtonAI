FAQ

How does Proton AI help my business?

It shows which customers, products, regions and people your results depend on most, so you can protect the right accounts, back under-rated growth drivers and spot concentration risk before it turns into a loss.

How is this different from a normal dashboard?

Dashboards sort by size. Proton looks at how things connect and influence your results, so it can surface small items that matter more than their revenue suggests.

How does AI make the answers better?

The AI turns scores into a plain-language briefing built on your own names and numbers, answers follow-up questions, and says so when the data is too thin to support a claim, instead of giving generic advice.

Who is Proton AI for?

Sales leaders, finance and operations teams, and founders who want clear priorities from the reports they already have, without needing a data team.

What data works best?

Transaction-level tables with at least two category columns (customer, product, region, rep) and a numeric measure. Aggregated summaries give weaker rankings.

Is my data sent anywhere?

Parsing and ranking happen in your browser. When you ask for advice or chat, a compact summary of the top-ranked items is sent to the AI, not your raw rows.

Can I trust the recommendations?

Every ranking shows the numbers behind it, and Proton flags weak signals when data is thin. Treat results as decision support and check them against what you know about your business.
